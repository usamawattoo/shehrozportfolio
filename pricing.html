Pricing questions

Are these the final prices?

They are starting prices based on the amount of data. Very messy files, handwriting that is hard to read, or special formatting can change the price. I tell you the exact price before starting.

What counts as one record?

One record is one row of information, for example one customer, one product or one invoice line. If your file has many columns per row, tell me and I will quote accordingly.

What if my job is bigger than Premium?

Send me the details on WhatsApp and I will prepare a custom quote.

Can I start with a small sample?

Yes. You can send a small sample first so we can agree on the format, price and delivery time.

What does a round of corrections mean?

If I made a mistake in your file, I correct it. Each package includes a number of correction rounds for my mistakes. New requests that change the original job are quoted separately.

Not sure which package fits?

Send me a short description or a sample of your file on WhatsApp and I will recommend a package and confirm the price.
