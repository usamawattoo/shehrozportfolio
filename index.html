Frequently asked questions

What kinds of files can you work with?

Excel (.xlsx, .xls), CSV, Word, PDF, and photos or scans of documents. I deliver the finished work as Excel, CSV or Word, whichever you need.

How do I place an order?

Tap any Order on WhatsApp button. WhatsApp opens with your chosen service, package and price already typed. Send it, share your files, and we confirm the details together.

How do you keep the data accurate?

I review each file after entering the data and correct any mistakes before delivery. If you spot a mistake of mine afterwards, I fix it as part of your package.

Will my data stay private?

Yes. I use your files only for your project and do not share them with anyone else. Please see the Privacy Policy for details.

How are prices decided?

Prices start from the package that matches the size of your job. After I see your files or a small sample, I confirm the final price and delivery time with you before work begins.

How do I pay?

Payment method and terms are agreed with you on WhatsApp before the work starts.

Have data that needs sorting out?
