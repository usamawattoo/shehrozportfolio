Privacy Policy

How your information is handled when you use this website or hire me.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This website belongs to Shahroz Ali, a data entry specialist based in Pakistan. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

Information you give me

  • Contact form: your name, email address, optional phone number, selected service and message.
  • WhatsApp, email and phone: any details and files you choose to send me when you contact me or place an order.

How I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To complete the work you hire me for.
  • To communicate with you about your order.

I do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Your files and project data

Files you send for a project are used only to complete that project. I do not share them with other people. Please do not send passwords, bank card numbers or other highly sensitive information unless it is truly necessary for the work and we have agreed on it first.

Third-party services

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): contact form messages are passed through this service, which forwards them to my email inbox.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): when you tap a WhatsApp button, you leave this site and use WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts: this site loads fonts from Google, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies and does not run analytics or tracking scripts of its own.

How long I keep information

I keep messages and project files only as long as needed to complete your work and answer follow-up questions. You can ask me to delete your files and messages at any time.

Your choices

You can ask me what information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Contact me using the details below.
